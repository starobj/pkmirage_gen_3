## Technical

### Stat Potions

#### Non-volatile Stats

Potions that boost **non-volatile stats**, such as: `HP`, `ATK`, `DEF`, `SPATK`, `SPDEF`, and `SPEED` work as follows:

| Stage   | Multiplier    | Increase    |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| +1      | 1.5x          | +50%        |
| +2      | 2.0x          | +100%       |
| +3      | 2.5x          | +150%       |
| +4      | 3.0x          | +200%       |
| +5      | 3.5x          | +250%       |
| +6      | 4.0x          | +300%       |

#### Volatile Stats

Potions that boost **volatile stats**, such as: `ACCURACY` and `EVASION` work as follows:

| Stage   | Multiplier    | Increase    |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| +1      | 1.33x         | +33%        |
| +2      | 1.66x         | +66%        |
| +3      | 2.0x          | +100%       |
| +4      | 2.33x         | +133%       |
| +5      | 2.66x         | +166%       |
| +6      | 3.0x          | +200%       |
